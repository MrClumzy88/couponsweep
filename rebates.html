Rebates

Matches your store receipts to Ibotta, Checkout 51, Fetch and coupons.com.

    Before you start. CouponSweep is unofficial and not affiliated with any store or app. Use it on your own accounts, at your own risk.

    How it works

    1. Sign in at albertsons.com (or Safeway, Tom Thumb… one account covers them all).
    2. Press Find my rebates. CouponSweep reads your receipts and this week's offers. It takes a minute or two the first time.
    3. Work down the To do list. Each item has one button.
