CouponSweep

How to clip all Walgreens digital coupons at once (free)

Stop pressing Clip one coupon at a time. CouponSweep clips every available Walgreens digital coupons for you, using the account you're already signed in with.

Get CouponSweep (free)

Steps

  1. Install CouponSweep in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
  2. Sign in at walgreens.com the way you normally do.
  3. Click the CouponSweep button in the toolbar, check Walgreens, and click Clip.
  4. Leave the tab open until the panel in the corner says it's done.

To keep up with new coupons, turn on Run once a day automatically and pick a time. You'll get a short summary when it's done.

How it works

It presses the page's own Clip buttons, so Walgreens' own code sends every request. It goes one coupon at a time with short pauses, and stops with a plain message if Walgreens says you're signed out, asks for a human check, or starts refusing requests.

Walgreens accounts hold up to 200 clipped coupons, so the biggest dollar amounts are clipped first.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. CouponSweep is free and open source, with no account and no subscription.

Does it need my password?

No. You sign in to walgreens.com yourself, the normal way. CouponSweep works inside that page and only talks to Walgreens.

Can it clip new coupons every day?

Yes. Turn on Run once a day automatically in the extension and pick a time. Chrome needs to be open then, and you need to stay signed in.

Will it spend anything?

No. Clipping only makes a coupon available at checkout.

All supported stores
