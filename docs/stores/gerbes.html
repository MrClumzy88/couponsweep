CouponSweep

How to clip all Gerbes digital coupons at once (free)

Stop pressing Clip one coupon at a time. CouponSweep clips every available Gerbes digital coupons for you, using the account you're already signed in with.

Get CouponSweep (free)

Steps

  1. Install CouponSweep in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
  2. Sign in at gerbes.com the way you normally do.
  3. Click the CouponSweep button in the toolbar, check Gerbes, and click Clip.
  4. Leave the tab open until the panel in the corner says it's done.

To keep up with new coupons, turn on Run once a day automatically and pick a time. You'll get a short summary when it's done.

How it works

It reads Kroger's own coupon list and sends the same clip request the site's Clip button sends. It goes one coupon at a time with short pauses, and stops with a plain message if Gerbes says you're signed out, asks for a human check, or starts refusing requests.

Kroger cards hold about 250 coupons. CouponSweep clips the most valuable ones first, so a full card has the best coupons on it.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. CouponSweep is free and open source, with no account and no subscription.

Does it need my password?

No. You sign in to gerbes.com yourself, the normal way. CouponSweep works inside that page and only talks to Gerbes.

Can it clip new coupons every day?

Yes. Turn on Run once a day automatically in the extension and pick a time. Chrome needs to be open then, and you need to stay signed in.

Does it work for every Kroger store?

Yes. Every Kroger banner shares one coupon system and CouponSweep supports all of them.

Will it spend anything?

No. Clipping only makes a coupon available at checkout.

Other Kroger stores

The same works on: Kroger, Ralphs, Fry's, King Soopers, Fred Meyer, Smith's, QFC, Dillons, City Market, Baker's, Jay C, Pay Less, Mariano's, Pick 'n Save, Metro Market, Foods Co, Food 4 Less.
